NOTE: the Keycloak CRDs are regular chart resources: `helm install` and
`helm upgrade` create and update them automatically. They are annotated with
"helm.sh/resource-policy: keep", so `helm uninstall` does NOT delete them
(this protects existing Keycloak resources from cascading deletion).
To remove the CRDs manually:

  kubectl delete crd keycloaks.k8s.keycloak.org keycloakrealmimports.k8s.keycloak.org keycloakoidcclients.k8s.keycloak.org keycloaksamlclients.k8s.keycloak.org
