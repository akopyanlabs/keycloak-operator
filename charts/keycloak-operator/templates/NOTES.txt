NOTE: the CRDs are installed from the chart's `crds/` directory. Helm applies
them on `helm install` but never upgrades or deletes them on subsequent
operations. When upgrading the chart to a new operator version, apply the
updated CRDs manually, e.g.:

  kubectl apply -f https://raw.githubusercontent.com/keycloak/keycloak-operator/<tag>/kubernetes/

See https://helm.sh/docs/chart_best_practices/custom_resource_definitions/ for details.
